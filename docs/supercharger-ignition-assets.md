## Animation

The assembly appears at 0.9 seconds behind the ACE logo. A single finite GSAP
rotation accelerates the impeller through 900 degrees over 3.3 seconds. The
outer casing stays stationary. A silver-green sweep crosses the assembly at
3.05 seconds, synchronized with the existing logo sweep. The assembly fades
by 4.3 seconds and the curtain lifts at 4.45 seconds. The existing six-second
timeline, measured navbar docking and reload-to-top behavior are preserved.

There is no continuous spin loop, new canvas, live 3D renderer, audio or video.
The rotor transform is cleared on finish, navigation, resize and cancellation.
Reduced motion, no JavaScript and direct section links bypass the effect.
An image-load error hides the entire decorative assembly without delaying the
logo or trapping the visitor behind the curtain.

# Section Bike Assets

Created November 13, 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool. The existing
`bike-front.webp` is the reference for the same synthesized carbon/silver/green
motorcycle. These are generated H2R-inspired interpretations, not official
Kawasaki photographs or product specifications. The illuminated red tail is a
cinematic design interpretation; no affiliation is claimed.

The official gallery was consulted for perspective inspiration, not copied:
https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/motorcycle/ninja/hypersport/ninja-h2r/2025-ninja-h2r-abs

All final project assets live in `public/assets/motorcycle/h2r/`. The original
PNG outputs remain in Codex's generated-images directory. The project script
`scripts/prepare-section-bikes.mjs` encodes the selected images at 1600 x 1000
as WebP with their alpha preserved. No background-removal heuristic is applied.

## Final Prompt Set

### bike-rear-quarter.webp

Experience / looking back through the career journey.

Use case: product-mockup camera-angle variation. Asset: transparent motorcycle backdrop for the Experience section of an existing racing portfolio. Reference image: the supplied transparent carbon-fiber/silver and green H2R-inspired motorcycle. Create a distinctly NEW CAMERA VIEW of the SAME motorcycle from its REAR LEFT THREE-QUARTER at a slightly elevated camera angle. Show the entire motorcycle with the broad rear racing tire nearest camera, seat, sharply sculpted tail, green trellis frame, carbon winglets, polished silver panels, windshield and front wheel receding toward upper left. The motorcycle still faces LEFT. Maintain the same materials, proportions, green wheel stripes and design identity as reference, not a new model. Strong clean white rim lighting and a thin green accent light illuminate the carbon and machined parts so the bike remains clearly visible over a dark webpage. High-end crisp photorealistic automotive studio product photography, not a low-poly render. Center the complete bike within a wide landscape 8:5 transparent-alpha canvas with a little safe padding, no clipped tires/tail. Genuinely transparent background and gaps between spokes/frame, dark bike surfaces remain opaque. No studio, ground, reflection, cast shadow, rider, scenery, added words, watermarks, border, or logos. This illustrates a career journey by looking back along the bike.

### bike-cockpit.webp

About / the person behind the controls.

Use case: product-mockup camera-angle variation. Asset: transparent motorcycle backdrop for the About section of a racing-themed developer portfolio, the personal rider's-eye perspective. Reference: supplied transparent carbon/silver/green H2R-inspired motorcycle. Create a genuinely different photograph of the SAME bike viewed from ABOVE AND BEHIND THE SEAT, looking forward over the sculpted glossy carbon-fiber fuel tank into the cockpit, clip-on handlebars, steering controls, forks, small instrument display and tinted windshield. A near overhead diagonal composition, pointing from lower right toward upper left. The close-up should show tactile carbon weave, silver machined fasteners, green trellis frame glimpses and premium black grips. Crop the motorcycle naturally at the lower seat edge, not a complete side view. NO rider or hands. Light the instrument display in restrained pale cyan/white, with clean soft white rim lighting and a green rim along one edge; keep components bright and legible against dark. Center this isolated tank/cockpit assembly on a wide 8:5 landscape genuinely transparent-alpha canvas with transparent space around its silhouette, no background, floor, cast shadow, scenery, fog, gradients, border, words, watermarks or added logos. Photorealistic precision automotive editorial photography. Same exact motorcycle design identity and materials as reference. This image should evoke the person behind the controls rather than repeat the front/side photograph.

### bike-rear-signal.webp

Contact / an illuminated open signal.

Use case: product-mockup camera-angle variation. Asset: transparent motorcycle backdrop for CONTACT / LET'S BUILD SOMETHING BEYOND in a racing developer portfolio. Reference supplied motorcycle is the exact design identity: black carbon-fiber and polished silver body, green trellis frame and green wheel trim. Create a NEW rear-facing view of the SAME H2R-inspired motorcycle, seen almost directly from BEHIND with a slight rear-right offset and low camera. A sculpted carbon tail with a vivid narrow RED illuminated tail-light, broad black racing rear tire, green rim edges, rear suspension/frame and silver exhaust visible; cockpit and front end recede beyond the seat. The rear signal is the luminous focal point, a little controlled red rim light against crisp white/green studio highlights. High-end photorealistic product photography, readable precise surfaces, not a dark silhouette or game model. Complete rear tire and tail safely inside a wide landscape 8:5 transparent-alpha canvas. Center the isolated bike with enough transparent side space; no floor, background, ground reflection, smoke, fog, halo orb, scene, rider, plate, words, labels, watermark or added logos. Preserve opaque dark bodywork and transparent gaps. The red lit tail is a cinematic interpretation, not an official product-specification photo. This is the last section's 'open communication channel' image and must feel visually distinct from the front and side images.

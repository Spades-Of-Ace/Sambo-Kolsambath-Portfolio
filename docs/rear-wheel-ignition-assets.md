## Animation

The rear wheel appears behind the ACE logo at 0.9 seconds and accelerates
through 900 degrees over 3.3 seconds. The rotation origin is aligned with the
artwork's axle. The tire and rim remain intact; no fixed swingarm, brake caliper
or impeller casing is included in the rotating layer.

A silver-green light sweep runs at 3.05 seconds in sync with the logo sweep.
Its alpha mask shares the wheel's rotating wrapper, so light stays registered
to the tire, rim and spokes rather than illuminating their empty spaces. The
wheel fades out by 4.3 seconds, before the curtain lifts at 4.45 seconds. The
six-second startup, measured logo-to-navbar handoff and reload-to-top behavior
are preserved.

The animation is finite and cleaned up on completion or skip. It adds no live
3D renderer, animation loop, video or audio. Reduced motion, no JavaScript and
direct section links bypass startup; an image-load error hides the decorative
wheel without blocking access to the portfolio.
